title: Handoff notes
description: Write notes that let another agent or person pick up this work without asking questions.
tags: [handoff, documentation]
---

I am {{agent.name}}, and I am handing off the work on {{branch}} in {{folder}}. Write handoff notes for whoever picks it up next.

Cover, in this order:

1. The goal, in one or two sentences.
2. What is done, with the commits or files that show it.
3. What is left, as a checklist in the order it should happen.
4. Decisions already made and why, so they are not re-argued.
5. Anything fragile: failing tests, workarounds, open questions, and who can answer them.

Keep it short enough to read in two minutes. Link to code and issues rather than restating them.
